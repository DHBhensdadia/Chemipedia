Periods

Left to right

A period is a row. Crossing one, each element adds a proton to its nucleus and an electron to the same outer shell, so a row runs from a metal that gives electrons away to a gas that would rather take one.

Read how the periods filled

Groups

Top to bottom

A group is a column. Its elements share the number of electrons in their outer shell, which is why they behave alike: the halogens all want one more electron, and the noble gases already have all they need.
